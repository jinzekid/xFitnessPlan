English

Fitness Plan respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

  • Training plans and records you enter in the app (stored locally on your device).
  • Subscription and purchase status (handled and provided by Apple).

How We Use Information

  • To display and manage your training plans and records in the app.
  • To unlock subscription features and provide support.

Data Storage

Your training data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your personal training data to our servers.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple subscription and payment services. Transaction data is handled by Apple and subject to Apple’s privacy policy.
